Also on Monday's agenda

  • Façade grant: staff ask council to direct that a by-law be prepared so the City can enter into a grant agreement for front façade work on the storefront of Antiques on the Side, on Talbot Street. The project was approved on April 28, 2026. The work is complete and cost about $6,858, and the report says it is eligible for a heritage façade and building improvement grant for the full amount.
  • Minor variance deferred: notice that the Committee of Adjustment has deferred a request about driveway width, a landscaped setback and a privacy fence at a residential property.
  • Proclamation requests: International Gastric Cancer Awareness Day on November 30, with the Jumbo monument lit in periwinkle, and Eczema Awareness Month in November, requested by the Eczema Society of Canada, with Jumbo lit in blue on November 20.
  • Conservation authorities: notices of the Catfish Creek and Kettle Creek conservation authorities' final 2027 budgets and levies.
  • Liquor licence: Winner: Player One has applied to the Alcohol and Gaming Commission of Ontario for a liquor licence for its indoor area at 121-417 Wellington Street. Council may make representation to the Commission.
  • By-laws: parking and traffic changes (no stopping on Scott Street; no parking on John Street, Queen Street, Fifth Avenue and Manitoba Street; speed limits on South Edgeware Road), and the by-law removing part-lot control for lots in Millers Pond Phase 6, which council directed be prepared on September 21.
