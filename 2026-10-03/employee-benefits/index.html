City employee benefits contracts up for award

Agenda item 19.d

Council will be asked on Monday to award the City's employee and retiree group benefits contracts from January 1, 2027. Staff's report says the carrier search (a request for proposals, or RFP) delivers an overall 10.4% cost reduction, or $747,648 a year, while short-term disability rates rise 19.8%, or $7,306 a month.

Staff recommend that council award the accidental death and dismemberment contract to AIG Insurance, and the life, dependent life, short-term disability, long-term disability, extended health care and dental care contracts to Manulife Financial. These are the City's current carriers. The report says the City's last search for carriers was in 2021, and that quotes for all benefits were received from Manulife, Sun Life and Desjardins.

The report's table lists each benefit's "Rate Change (%)" and monthly dollar change. Short-term disability is the only one of the table's seven lines that rises, and it carries a 12-month rate guarantee.

Staff also ask council to approve the consultant's recommendation to move short-term disability, extended health care and dental benefits from "Retention Accounting" to "Administrative Services Only" (ASO). The report says that under Retention Accounting the City is already responsible for any deficit incurred, and that under ASO the insurer holds none of the reserve funds the report lists, because the policyholder is fully responsible for paying any deficit.

In November 2024, council approved a renewal of its Manulife benefit program for extended health care, dental, short-term disability, life insurance and long-term disability, "effective January 1, 2025, for a period of one (1) year." In December 2025, it received a report on the 2026 renewal.
