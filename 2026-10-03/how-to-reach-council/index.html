How to reach council

Council meets Monday, October 5 at 5 p.m. in council chambers and on Zoom.

Email. The City publishes these addresses for the members of council:

  • Mayor Joe Preston: mayor@stthomas.ca
  • Councillor Earl Taylor: etaylor@stthomas.ca
  • Councillor Gary Clarke: gclarke@stthomas.ca
  • Councillor Jim Herbert: jherbert@stthomas.ca
  • Councillor Jeff Kohler: jkohler67@live.com
  • Councillor Joan Rymal: jrymal@stthomas.ca
  • Councillor Lori Baldwin-Sands: lbaldwin-sands@stthomas.ca
  • Councillor Rose Gibson: rgibson@stthomas.ca
  • Councillor Steve Wookey: swookey@stthomas.ca

Questions about this edition. Staff or the clerk can answer the questions listed in the Questions to ask block above. Readers can also ask any member of council, before Monday's meeting, to put those questions to staff at that meeting.

Speaking to council. To speak as a deputation, send a signed letter of request to the City Clerk's Department with your name, address, telephone number and the topic, with an original signature. The City says it must be delivered by 12 p.m. on the Tuesday before the meeting. A deputation gets at most 10 minutes, including questions. For Monday's meeting that Tuesday was September 29, so that deadline has passed.

Written comments. The City's pages do not say how residents can send written comments for an agenda item. Council's Procedural By-law says correspondence intended to be presented to council must be signed by its author and becomes part of the public record.

Customer Service, part of the City Clerk's Department, is at City Hall, Level 2, 545 Talbot Street, Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Phone: (519) 631-1680, the City's number. Email: customerservice@stthomas.ca.
