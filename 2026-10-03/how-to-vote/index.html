How voting works in the October 26 election

Agenda item 19.c

A report from the Deputy City Clerk on Monday's council agenda, for information, sets out how voting works in the October 26 municipal election.

  • How to vote: electors can vote by internet or telephone, 24 hours a day, from 10 a.m. on October 13 to 8 p.m. on October 26.
  • Who can vote: the report says you must be 18 or older on October 26; a Canadian citizen; a resident, an owner or tenant of land in St. Thomas, or the spouse of such an owner or tenant; and not prohibited from voting or otherwise prohibited by law.
  • The voters' list: a name must be on it to vote. Check at https://stthomas.voterservices.ca. If you are not on the list, or need a correction (address, school support), submit Form EL15 to the City Clerk's Department: bring it, signed, with identification showing your name and qualifying address, to the Clerk's Office or a Voter Assistance Centre.
  • Voter Information Letters, with your PIN, are to arrive by mail by October 7.
  • Voter Assistance Centres:
    • Thursday, October 15, 1 to 7 p.m., Elgin Centre, 417 Wellington Street
    • Wednesday, October 21, noon to 4 p.m., St. Thomas Seniors' Centre, 225 Chestnut Street
    • Saturday, October 24, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., Elgin Centre
    • Monday, October 26, 10 a.m. to 8 p.m., St. Thomas Public Library, 153 Curtis Street
  • Institutions: special voting hours will be available in institutions where 20 or more beds are occupied by persons who are disabled, chronically ill, or infirm, and in long-term care or retirement homes where 50 or more beds are occupied. The report gives no dates.

The report says Simply Voting provides the voting platform, and that once a ballot is stored, no one can determine how an elector voted.

How to take part

  • Check now that your name is on the voters' list at https://stthomas.voterservices.ca. The report says a name must be on the list to vote.
  • If your Voter Information Letter has not arrived by October 7, contact the Voter Help Desk weekdays from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. at 519-631-1680 (press 0) or customerservice@stthomas.ca, or go in person to the City Clerk's Department, Level 2, City Hall, 545 Talbot Street, Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.

Questions to ask

  • When and where are the special voting hours for electors in institutions where 20 or more beds are occupied by persons who are disabled, chronically ill, or infirm, or in long-term care or retirement homes where 50 or more beds are occupied? The report says they will be available but gives no dates.
  • Is there a deadline to be added to, or corrected on, the voters' list during the October 13 to 26 voting period? The report gives none.
  • Besides voting by internet or telephone and the special voting hours in institutions, is any other way of voting available?
