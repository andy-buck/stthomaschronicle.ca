Council to receive a report on a new Noise Control By-law

Agenda item 19.a

Council will consider a staff recommendation on Monday that report CC-20-26 on a new Noise Control By-law "be received for information" and that the by-law "be brought forward for Council’s consideration." The recommendation does not ask council to adopt the by-law. The draft leaves its effective date blank.
