Road closures planned for three November events

Agenda items 11.a, 11.b, 11.c

Council will consider road closures for three events in November:

  • Remembrance Day, Wednesday, November 11: Moore Street between Talbot Street and Centre Street, 8 a.m. to 1 p.m. The St. Thomas Legion, Branch 41 is organizing a ceremony at the new Veterans Memorial Garden, with a police-escorted parade from 24 John St. Access to the Moore Street parking lot from Centre Street would stay open, and staff say a temporary transit detour will be posted in advance.
  • Light up Talbot, Friday, November 13: Mondamin Street from Curtis Street to Talbot Street, and Talbot Street from East Street to Horton Street, 4 to 8:30 p.m. Last year's approved closure was 4:30 to 9:30 p.m. Staff also recommend an exemption from the Animal Control By-law for horse and wagon rides and farm animals for a nativity scene. On the morning of the event, pylons would go up around City Hall along Talbot and Mondamin streets to prohibit on-street parking. A temporary transit detour will be posted in advance.
  • Santa Claus Parade, Saturday, November 21: First Avenue from Talbot Street to Redan Street, 3 to 8:30 p.m., and Talbot Street from First Avenue to William Street, 5:30 to 8:30 p.m. The Optimist Club of St. Thomas sponsors the parade. It runs west along Talbot Street and officially ends at Elgin Street. Parking would be restricted on both sides of the parade route from 5 to 8:30 p.m. Staff recommend that access for emergency vehicles be maintained at all times through the closures, and the east, northbound lane of First Avenue would be available to them.

Last year's (2025) versions of all three events were approved by council in October 2025 by voice vote, after Committee of the Whole; this year's have not been decided. The reports do not give the detour routes.
