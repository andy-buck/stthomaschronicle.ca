The St. Thomas Chronicle, 2026-10-10

Council meets the day after Thanksgiving Monday, October 12, with more on the table than turkey: a sports dome, apartment plans, a contract and a public art rule.

In this edition:

  • Staff recommend backing a provincial bid for an indoor sports dome, about $14 million, and a $90,800-plus-HST study.
  • Staff recommend approving draft plans for three Moore Street apartment buildings.
  • A $1.2 million single-source contract for Yarmouth Yards site work.
  • A proposed policy on adding items to public art.
  • Also on the agenda: a strategic plan update, a notice of motion and by-laws.
  • The week at council: a look back at Monday's decisions.
  • How to reach council.
